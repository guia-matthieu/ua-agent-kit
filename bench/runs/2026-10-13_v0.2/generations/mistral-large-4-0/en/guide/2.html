Validation follows Universal Acceptance: domain names are checked with IDNA2008 / UTS #46 (TLDs are not limited to 2–4 letters and may be punycode A-labels such as .xn--p1ai), and email addresses follow RFC 6530/6531, so internationalized addresses such as josé.dupont@société.fr are accepted. TLD existence is deliberately not checked — that would require the IANA root-zone list.
