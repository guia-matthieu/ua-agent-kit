Folio · Membership Received

Folio is a small society of designers, editors and engineers who still believe in the well-made page. Members receive the quarterly dispatch, the full archive, and a standing invitation to our print days.

We admit new members twice a year, by hand.

Password must include

  • Eight characters or more
  • Upper & lowercase letters
  • At least one number
  • At least one symbol, e.g. ! ? #

All four fields are required.
