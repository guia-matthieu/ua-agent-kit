# Forms the four reviewers wrote (2026-10-07 and 08)

Copied as they left them from the reviewers' scratch folders: the pages (`*.html`) each review ran through `checkForm`, and the small scripts that ran them. `1/` to `4/` are the four reviews, in order; the findings they support are summed up in `../README.md`.

The scripts are kept under `.mjs.txt`: they are a record, not part of the kit, and are not held to its lint. They import the runner by the absolute path of the machine they were written on and are kept as a record, not as something to run as it is: point the import at `src/form-runner.mjs` of this repository first. The copies of the repository the reviewers made at earlier commits, and their mutation folders, were not kept.
